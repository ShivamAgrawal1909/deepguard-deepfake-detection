Deepfake Awareness

What are Deepfakes?

Deepfakes are synthetic media created using artificial intelligence to replace a person's likeness with someone else's. They can be used to create convincing but fake videos and images.

How to Identify Deepfakes

  • Unnatural blinking patterns or lack of blinking
  • Inconsistent lighting on the face vs. background
  • Blurry or distorted edges around the face
  • Unusual skin texture or color inconsistencies
  • Audio-visual synchronization issues
  • Frequency-domain artifacts detectable by AI analysis

Why Use DeepGuard?

Our system goes beyond visual inspection by analyzing the frequency patterns and temporal features that deepfake generation algorithms leave behind, providing scientific evidence of manipulation.

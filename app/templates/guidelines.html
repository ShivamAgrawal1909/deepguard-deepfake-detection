Video Upload Guidelines

Supported Formats

MP4, AVI, MOV, MKV, WEBM

Requirements

  • Maximum file size: 100 MB
  • Video must contain readable frames
  • Minimum resolution recommended: 480p
  • Faces should be visible for optimal detection

Image Detection

Supported image formats: JPG, JPEG, PNG, BMP, WEBP

Tips for Best Results

  • Use well-lit videos with clear facial features
  • Avoid heavily compressed or low-quality footage
  • Shorter clips (under 2 minutes) process faster
  • Ensure the subject's face is clearly visible
